Kirana Billing

ಶ್ರೀಧರ್ ಕಿರಾಣಿ ಸ್ಟೋರ್ಸ್ — billing app for the shop counter

⬇ Download app (APK)
Version 1.0.0 · Android 7.0 and above
  1. Tap the green button above. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If the phone warns about unknown apps, tap Settings and allow it for Chrome.
  3. Open the app. Pair the printer in Android Bluetooth settings first, then choose it in the app under Settings → Choose printer.
  4. Do a Test print — if it reads 1370 and the Kannada is clear, everything is right.
The app works fully offline — bills, items and history stay on the phone. No account or internet needed at the counter.
